Why we huddle

We use our own observations, staff feedback, and honest feedback to one another to strengthen Summer PD in real time. We celebrate what is working, name concerns quickly, and make values-aligned adjustments before the next PD day.

Ground the Huddle
Get a quick read of the leadership team, then reconnect to our values.
Round robin prompt
What is one word or phrase that describes how today landed?
Output: A shared read of the room.
0:04–0:12
8 min
Stage 1
Our Read
Start with our own leadership observations before we look at staff feedback.
1 Win
What worked today?
1 Concern
What needs attention?
1 Adjustment
What do we do differently?
Look across facilitation, engagement, pacing, clarity, logistics, energy, and the overall staff experience.
Output: The leadership team's initial assessment.
0:12–0:22
10 min
Stage 2
Staff Voice
Understand what staff are actually telling us. Sort the feedback into three buckets.
Celebrate
Working, keep going.
Watch
May need attention.
Adjust Now
Change before next PD.
Discussion question
What story is the staff feedback telling us?
Prioritize patterns and repeated feedback. Do not overreact to isolated comments.
Output: 2 to 3 major staff feedback trends.
0:22–0:30
8 min
Stage 3
People + Values
Name staff who need support, recognition, clarification, accountability, or follow-up.
  • 1What did we observe? Stick to observable evidence.
  • 2Why does it matter? Impact on the person, team, or PD experience.
  • 3Which value connects? Kid Focus, Radical Love, Excellence, Team, or Fun.
  • 4What is our leadership move? Decide the right follow-up.
  • 5Who owns it? Every follow-up gets a clear owner.
Also name staff who are living the values well and deserve recognition or affirming feedback.
Output: Staff follow-up and recognition list, with owners.
0:30–0:40
10 min
Stage 4
Mirror Check: Feedback to Each Other
Help each other get better as leaders during Summer PD. Everyone gives and receives both.
Affirming: "Keep doing..."
A behavior that helped the team or staff.
Adjusting: "Try tomorrow..."
A behavior that would make you more effective.
Affirming: When you __________, the impact was __________.
Adjusting: Tomorrow, I need you to __________ because __________.
Be specific Kind and direct Observable behaviors Receive with curiosity Clarifying questions welcome No need to defend on the spot
Output: One clear adjustment or reinforcement for each person.
0:40–0:46
6 min
Stage 5
Tomorrow's Moves
Turn reflection into action. Every adjustment needs an action, an owner, and a when.
Keep
What we absolutely continue.
Change
What we do differently.
Communicate
What staff need to know or feel.
Prioritize the few changes that will most improve the next PD experience.
Output: The next-day action plan. Stamp it below.
0:46–0:52
6 min
Housekeeping + Look Ahead
Clear the logistics and walk tomorrow's plan so no one leaves guessing.
Housekeeping
Announcements, reminders, deadlines, coverage, sign-outs.
Next Day Look-Ahead
Walk tomorrow's plan out loud.
  • 1Agenda and timing. What is tomorrow, and when.
  • 2Materials and setup. What must be printed, staged, or built.
  • 3Roles. Who is leading and who is supporting each part.
  • 4Ready tonight. Anything that has to happen before we leave.
Before we close
Is anyone unclear on their role or what has to be ready for tomorrow?
Output: Everyone clear on logistics and ready for tomorrow. Anything due tonight goes in Next Steps below.
0:52–0:55
3 min
All In Close
Rapid round robin. One observable commitment from each leader.
Public commitment
"Tomorrow, you will see me..."
Output: A public leadership commitment from every person.
